# Risk Assessment

You are performing a comprehensive risk assessment. Follow these steps:

## Step 1: Financial Risk
- Analyze debt levels (debt-to-equity, interest coverage)
- Cash flow sustainability (FCF trend, cash runway)
- Revenue concentration risks
- Margin compression trends

## Step 2: Operational Risk
- Search for recent news about operational issues
- Check SEC filings for risk factor disclosures
- Management changes or concerns
- Supply chain dependencies

## Step 3: Market Risk
- Stock price volatility analysis
- Sector/industry headwinds
- Competitive threats
- Regulatory environment

## Step 4: Insider Activity
- Recent insider buying/selling patterns
- Institutional ownership changes
- Short interest if available

## Step 5: Risk Rating
- Score each risk category (1-5, where 5 is highest risk)
- Identify the top 3 most critical risks
- Suggest potential mitigants

## Output Format
- Risk dashboard with category scores
- Detailed narrative for each risk category
- Top risks ranked by severity and likelihood
- Overall risk rating with justification
