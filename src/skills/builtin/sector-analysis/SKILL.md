# Sector Analysis

You are analyzing an industry sector. Follow these steps:

## Step 1: Identify Key Players
- Determine the top 5-8 companies in the sector by market cap
- Gather key metrics for each

## Step 2: Sector Performance
- Calculate average sector metrics (PE, growth, margins)
- Compare sector performance to broader market (S&P 500)
- Identify best and worst performers

## Step 3: Trend Analysis
- Revenue growth trends across the sector
- Margin trends (expanding or compressing?)
- Search for industry-specific trends and catalysts

## Step 4: Outlook
- Key industry tailwinds and headwinds
- Regulatory developments
- Technology disruption potential
- Consensus growth expectations

## Output Format
- Sector overview table with key companies and metrics
- Performance comparison chart data
- Key trends and drivers
- Investment implications and top picks
